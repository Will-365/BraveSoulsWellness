About
Brave Souls Wellness

Brave Souls Wellness Group Ltd is a registered wellness company based in Kigali, Rwanda. We provide structured fitness and wellness coaching that develops not only physical strength, but also mindset, discipline, and inner stability because lasting transformation starts from within.

We are founded and led by a husband and wife leadership team committed to building disciplined wellness systems that develop both body and spirit. We create structured wellness solutions that help individuals and organizations become stronger, healthier and more capable of living at their highest potential

Our Mission

To empower individuals and organizations to build healthier, stronger and more purposeful lives through structured wellness solutions, professional coaching, education and accountability.

Our Vision

To become a leading African wellness organization recognized for excellence, transformation, discipline and holistic wellbeing.

Brave Souls Executive Wellness & Performance

Healthy Body and Spirit. Better Performance.

Executive wellness designed around the reality of leadership.

Senior leaders carry enormous responsibility. Their days are filled with meetings, strategic decisions, deadlines, travel, team management and constant demands on their time.

Yet the people responsible for leading organizations often have the least time to prioritize their own wellbeing.

Brave Souls Wellness Group Ltd provides structured fitness, wellness and performance coaching designed specifically for executives and senior leadership teams.

We bring professional coaching directly to the executive — at the workplace, residence, private gym or another agreed location — so wellness can become part of the schedule rather than another demand on it.

We don't ask busy leaders to find time for wellness. We bring wellness to them.

Book an Executive Consultation
The executive wellness challenge

Leadership requires energy, focus and consistency.

However, senior professionals often face:

  • Demanding schedules
  • Long hours of meetings and decision-making
  • Extended periods of sitting
  • Limited time for structured exercise
  • Inconsistent wellness routines
  • Work-related pressure and fatigue
  • Frequent travel
  • Difficulty maintaining accountability
  • Wellness being postponed because of competing priorities

The challenge is often not a lack of knowledge.

TIME STRUCTURE CONSISTENCY ACCOUNTABILITY

Our program is designed around these realities.

Our solution

Wellness that fits the executive's life

Traditional fitness programs ask busy professionals to adjust their lives around a fixed schedule.

We take a different approach.

Brave Souls designs and delivers structured wellness sessions around the executive's schedule and organizational needs.

Sessions can be delivered:

  • Before the working day
  • During designated breaks
  • Between meetings
  • After working hours
  • At the organization's location
  • At an agreed fitness facility
  • Through one-on-one coaching
  • Through small executive-group sessions

The result is a professional, flexible and structured wellness experience designed for leadership.

Jean Pierre Mufukuzi
Coach Brave

Jean Pierre Mufukuzi, known professionally as Coach Brave, is a certified fitness trainer, wellness entrepreneur and founder of Brave Souls Wellness Group Ltd.

With a background in spinning, fitness coaching, business administration, Bible and theology, Jean Pierre brings together physical training, leadership, discipline and purpose in his approach to wellness.
